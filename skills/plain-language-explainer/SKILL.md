# Plain Language Explainer

## Steps
1. Find the main point. Say it first, in one sentence.
2. Use the four sections required by the project instructions.
3. Swap every hard word for an everyday word. If a term must stay, define it in parentheses.
4. End with the official-notice reminder required by the project instructions. Do not add a follow-up question or extra closing text.

## Rules
- Short sentences. Under 15 words when you can.
- Keep the meaning exactly. Never add facts that were not in the original.
- If it is a deadline or money, put it in **bold**.
- Do not add routine medical or legal disclaimers unless a specific safety concern makes one relevant.
- Preserve necessary safety warnings stated in the letter.
